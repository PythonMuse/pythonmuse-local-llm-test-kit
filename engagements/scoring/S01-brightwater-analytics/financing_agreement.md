## Stock Purchase Agreement

**Section 1.1 Sale and Issuance of Shares.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Company agreed to issue and sell, and the Purchasers agreed to purchase, an aggregate of **2,000,000 shares** of Series A Preferred Stock at a purchase price of $2.50 per share. All 2,000,000 shares were issued at the Closing.

**Section 1.2 Lead Purchaser.** Copperline Growth Fund I, L.P. (fictional).

## Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation, Article IV(B)

**Section 2.1 Liquidation Preference.** In the event of any liquidation, dissolution, winding up, or Deemed Liquidation Event, the holders of the Series A Preferred Stock shall be entitled to receive, before any payment to the holders of Common Stock, an amount per share equal to **one times (1x)** the Original Issue Price, plus any declared but unpaid dividends (the "Liquidation Preference").

**Section 2.2 Participation.** After payment of the full Liquidation Preference, the holders of the Preferred Stock shall not participate further in any remaining distribution (the Preferred Stock is **non-participating**). Each holder may instead elect to convert to Common Stock if that would yield a greater amount.

**Section 4.1 Right to Convert.** Each share of Series A Preferred Stock shall be convertible, at the option of the holder, at any time, into **one (1)** fully paid and non-assessable share(s) of Common Stock (a conversion ratio of **1:1**), subject to adjustment as provided in Section 4.4.

**Section 4.2 Automatic Conversion.** All outstanding shares of Series A Preferred Stock shall automatically convert into Common Stock at the then-effective conversion ratio upon the closing of a Qualified IPO.
